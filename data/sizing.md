# Size Guide — Nusa Threads

## How to measure
- Chest: measure around the fullest part of your chest, under the arms.
- Waist: measure around your natural waistline.
- Length: measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the hem.
Our fits are true to size. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit.

## T-shirts and shirts (unisex), chest width in cm
- S: 96–100 cm
- M: 101–106 cm
- L: 107–112 cm
- XL: 113–118 cm
- XXL: 119–124 cm

## Pants (unisex), waist in cm
- 28: 71–73 cm
- 30: 76–78 cm
- 32: 81–83 cm
- 34: 86–88 cm
- 36: 91–93 cm

## Fabric and care
Our T-shirts are 100% combed cotton (24s). Pants are 98% cotton, 2% elastane for stretch.
Wash cold (max 30°C), inside out. Do not tumble dry. Expect up to 3% shrinkage after the first wash.
