# Returns & Refunds — Nusa Threads

## Return window
You can return items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have their original tags attached.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Underwear and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Items marked "Final Sale"
- Gift cards
- Custom or personalised items

## How to start a return
1. Log in and go to "My Orders".
2. Select the order and click "Request Return".
3. Choose the items and the reason for return.
4. Print the return label we email you and drop the package at any JNE outlet.

Domestic return shipping is free. For international returns, the customer pays return shipping.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect your return. Original shipping fees are not refunded, unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.

## Exchanges
We offer free size exchanges within Indonesia, subject to stock availability. To exchange for a different size, start a return and choose "Exchange" as the reason. For a different product, return the item for a refund and place a new order.

## Faulty or wrong items
If you received a faulty or incorrect item, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will cover all shipping costs and send a replacement or full refund.
