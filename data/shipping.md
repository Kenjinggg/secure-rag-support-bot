# Shipping Policy — Nusa Threads

## Processing time
All orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays). Orders placed after 2:00 PM (GMT+7) are processed the next business day.

## Domestic shipping (Indonesia)
- Standard shipping (JNE Reguler): 2–5 business days, Rp 20.000 flat rate.
- Express shipping (JNE YES): 1–2 business days, Rp 45.000 flat rate.
- Free standard shipping on all domestic orders over Rp 500.000.

## International shipping
We ship to Malaysia, Singapore, Australia, the United States and the United Kingdom.
- International standard: 7–14 business days, USD 15 flat rate.
- International express (DHL): 3–5 business days, USD 35 flat rate.
- Free international standard shipping on orders over USD 150.
Customers are responsible for any import duties or taxes charged by their country.

We do not currently ship to any countries not listed above.

## Order tracking
Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number. You can also track your order from the "My Orders" page in your account. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show updates after the email is sent.

## Lost or delayed packages
If your tracking has not updated for more than 7 days, contact us and we will open an investigation with the courier. If a package is confirmed lost, we will send a replacement or issue a full refund — your choice.
