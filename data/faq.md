# Frequently Asked Questions — Nusa Threads

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, bank transfer (BCA, Mandiri, BNI), GoPay, OVO and ShopeePay. International customers can pay by Visa, Mastercard or PayPal.

## Can I change or cancel my order?
You can change or cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it by emailing us with your order number. After that, the order may already be processing and cannot be changed.

## Do you have a physical store?
No, Nusa Threads is an online-only store. We occasionally join pop-up markets in Jakarta — follow our Instagram @nusathreads for announcements.

## Do you offer discounts?
New subscribers to our newsletter get 10% off their first order. We do not offer student discounts at this time.

## How do I contact customer support?
Email support@nusathreads.example. Our team replies Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (GMT+7), usually within 1 business day.

## Do you restock sold-out items?
Popular items are usually restocked within 4–6 weeks. Click "Notify me" on the product page to get an email when it is back.

## Are your products ethically made?
All our clothing is made by our partner workshop in Bandung, which pays above the regional minimum wage. We publish a yearly report on our "About" page.
